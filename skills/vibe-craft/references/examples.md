# Contrastive Transformation Examples

Use these as reasoning demonstrations, not fixed taxonomies.

## “Make this cup cuter, but not childish”

Weak translation: “Increase cuteness from 5 to 8.”

Useful dimensions:

| Dimension | Candidate target |
| --- | --- |
| Proportion | Slightly wider body and shorter height, not toy-like |
| Contour | Softer corners without exaggerated roundness |
| Color | Warm low-saturation accent, avoiding candy colors |
| Expression | No cartoon face; friendliness comes from silhouette |
| Detail | One distinctive handle detail, otherwise restrained |

Contrastive directions:

- **Soft companion** — warmer, rounder, tactile; risk: can become juvenile.
- **Friendly minimal** — restrained form with one charming detail; recommended for “not childish.”
- **Playful collectible** — expressive color and character cues; rejected by the boundary.

Acceptance example: “At thumbnail size the silhouette feels friendlier than the current cup, while an adult could still place it in a professional workspace without it reading as a toy.”

## “Make this product website feel more premium”

Weak translation: add black, gradients, glass, and slow animation.

Useful dimensions:

| Dimension | Current question | Possible target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hierarchy | Is everything competing? | One dominant promise and one primary action |
| Restraint | How many decorative treatments appear? | Fewer effects, stronger spacing and alignment |
| Proof | Are claims generic? | Specific product evidence near each claim |
| Typography | Does type express confidence? | Controlled scale, tracking, and line length |
| Motion | Is movement demonstrating quality or delaying work? | Fast purposeful feedback; no decorative loops |
| Finish | Are small states inconsistent? | Cohesive hover, focus, loading, and error behavior |

Alternative reading to challenge: the person may mean “more trustworthy” rather than “more luxurious.” Those targets lead to different copy, proof, and visual decisions.

Acceptance example: “A first-time visitor can identify the product, principal benefit, evidence, and next action within one viewport; decorative treatments no longer substitute for specificity.”

## “Make this writing sound natural and professional”

Weak translation: use a generic friendly tone.

Useful dimensions:

| Dimension | Possible target |
| --- | --- |
| Rhythm | Mix sentence lengths without artificial fragments |
| Vocabulary | Domain-specific plain words, minimal corporate filler |
| Confidence | Direct claims with calibrated uncertainty |
| Warmth | Human acknowledgment without forced enthusiasm |
| Structure | Lead with the outcome, then necessary reasoning |
| Evidence | Replace adjectives with concrete facts or examples |

Contrastive directions:

- **Expert concise** — compact, precise, assumes context.
- **Trusted guide** — explanatory and warm without becoming chatty.
- **Corporate polished** — smooth but abstract; often conflicts with “natural.”

Acceptance example: “A domain reader can identify the claim and evidence without translating jargon, and no sentence sounds written solely to appear impressive.”
